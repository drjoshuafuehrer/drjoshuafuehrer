BPMN for the App Generation
Lesson 1: Connect to an App with OneDrive

Course Material
---------------
A connector allows a BPMN model to interact with an external application or service.

In this lesson:
- OneDrive is the interface.
- "Get file by path" is the operation.
- filePath is process data supplied to the operation.
- retrievedFile is process data that receives the result.

Key idea:
The process defines the work. The connector defines how the app performs it.

Example question:
What is the specific action that the process asks OneDrive to perform?

Answer:
Get file by path.
